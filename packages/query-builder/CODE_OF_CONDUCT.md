# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Ngandu projects a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and empathetic.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Focus discussion on what is best for the project and its community.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, trolling, and other unprofessional conduct are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

Report conduct concerns privately to bernard@ngandu.dev. Reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and with respect for the reporter's privacy and safety.

This policy is based on the principles of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
